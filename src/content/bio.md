I am a master's student in Astronomy at Beijing Normal University, advised by
Prof. He Gao. My research focuses on fast radio bursts, plasma propagation
effects, and numerical simulations of high-energy transient sources.

## Research Interests

- Electromagnetic wave–plasma interactions
- Fast radio bursts and radio-wave propagation
- Particle-in-cell simulations
- Magnetic reconnection
- Relativistic collisionless shocks

## Current Work

I contribute to the EMPI high-performance computing software package and
participate in the FAST FRB Key Science Project. My previous work includes
particle-in-cell simulations, the Tiange / GRID07 satellite project, and
supernova searches in ZTF data.
