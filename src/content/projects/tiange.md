The **Tiange Project** is a satellite program to observe gamma-ray bursts (GRBs).
From 2023 to 2025, I served as **Student Leader for Beijing Normal University's
GRID07 satellite**.

- **Collaborators:** Chenyu Wang and others
- **Advisors:** Lin Lin and He Gao

My research experience also includes basic satellite data processing pipelines.
