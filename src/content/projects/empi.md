**ELECTROMAGNETIC WAVE-PLASMA INTERACTION (EMPI)** is a high-performance
computing (HPC) software package designed to simulate the complex interactions
between electromagnetic waves and plasma, focusing on spreading phenomena.

Authors: Nan Xu and Songyu Shen.

Since spring 2025, I have worked on numerical studies of **plasma-induced
propagation effects on radio signals**, collaborating with Nan Xu and advised
by He Gao, Yuan-Pei Yang, and Bing Zhang.

My current manuscripts address **magnetized media** and **numerical accuracy
and resolution requirements**. Both are in preparation; see the
[publications page](/publications) for their status and author lists.

I presented *The EMPI Code for Plasma-induced Effects on Radio Waves: Code
Implementation and Performance* at the COSPAR International Conference in
Italy in August 2026.

Project reference: [The EMPI Code for Plasma-induced Effects on Radio Waves. I.
Nonmagnetized Media and Applications to Fast Radio
Bursts](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/adc92d)
