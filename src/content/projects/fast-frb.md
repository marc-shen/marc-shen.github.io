title: FAST FRB Key Science Project
description: Research on fast radio bursts with the FAST radio telescope, since April 2026.
---

Since April 2026, I have participated in the **FAST FRB Key Science Project**,
using the FAST radio telescope to study fast radio bursts (FRBs).

This work complements my numerical research on the plasma propagation effects
that influence radio signals from these transient sources.
