From 2021 to 2023, I worked on searching and selecting supernovae in
**Zwicky Transient Facility (ZTF)** data to study specific core-collapse models.
This work formed a **Beijing Municipal Undergraduate Research Project**.

- **Collaborators:** Yulin Li and Junming He
- **Advisor:** He Gao

I subsequently attended the ZTF Summer School in summer 2024.
